BUST
Each die has a red Bust side.\nRolling Bust side reduces your Bust meter.\n\nIf Bust meter goes to 0 you WIPE.\nAll unbanked points are gone, turn ends.
ALL BUST - INSTANT WIPE
The tricky part!\n\nIf all dice you rolled at once show Bust,\nyou WIPE instantly, regardless of the Bust meter.\n\nExample: 3 out of 3 dice rolled Bust
BANKING
BANK to lock in your points and refill the Bust meter.\nEach BANK ends your Turn.
TURN
Each level you get limited Turns.\nTurns end when you BANK or WIPE.\nWhen you run out of Turns the game is over.\nBust meter and Power charge refill each turn.
SCORING
Select dice that form a combo and score them.\nPoints x Mult = Score.\nBigger combos score exponentially more.\nScore multiple combos in one Turn to build Momentum for extra Points and Mult
TRINKETS, SPELLS & SNACKS
- Trinkets are passive, they trigger automatically.\n- Spells are one time use!\n- Snacks are dragged onto a die to add special effects.
CHARACTER POWER
Each character has a unique Power. Use them to modify dice, reduce risk and get better combos.\n\nPower recharges each turn or with die side effects and items.
BASIC TACTICS
The more dice you roll at once the bigger chance of any Bust, but smaller chance of All Bust.\n\nRerolling a single die is ballsy! Prioritize rerolling blank dice. Scoring a single big combo is better than few pairs.
